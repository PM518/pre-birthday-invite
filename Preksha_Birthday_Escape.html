Important mission briefing

Pack your bags, birthday girl.

One night means we don't need to carry our entire lives with us. Keep it cute, comfortable and easy.

Definitely pack

  • Comfortable outfit for the drive
  • One nice / cute dinner outfit
  • Swimwear
  • Change of clothes for Day 2
  • Nightwear
  • Underwear & essentials
  • Toiletries & skincare
  • Phone + charger / power bank

Nice to have

  • Your favourite perfume
  • Sunglasses
  • Comfortable outdoor footwear
  • Camera / Polaroid if you want
  • Hair ties / clips
  • A book or Kindle
  • A playlist downloaded offline
  • One outfit you feel ridiculously pretty in
Do NOT pack: work laptop, unnecessary stress, Monday energy, or the feeling that we have to “do” something every minute. Also: leave a little room in the bag. You never know.
